Sharing an interesting research video I saw today: it argued that “human nature is good” and “human nature is evil” may be two expressions of the same underlying mechanism. People can tell within 200 milliseconds whether the person in front of them belongs to their in-group. When someone is framed as one of us, oxytocin strengthens protective, self-sacrificing, and giving behavior toward that person; when someone is framed as outside the group, the tendency turns toward exclusion. It made me think about the dominant Western values being exported today, which emphasize individual thought and self-realization. Maybe those values compress the group a person can recognize as “one of us” down toward the limit of an individual’s social capacity, which the video described as about 150 people, leaving too little collective identity. Social problems around us, such as selfishness, declining birth rates, loss of expectation for the future, and weaker group consciousness, are certainly not caused by a single factor. Still, I want to leave this possible cause here so I do not forget it later.
